| Q My Context and Coach handlers crash on my PowerMac. What's going on?   A Not to worry. You just need to upgrade your Apple Guide extension to Apple Guide 1.2.7 or greater. Apple Guide 1.2.7 fixed problems with mixed mode handling of installed coach and context handlers. The crashing occurred on PowerMacs running native Apple Guide 1.2.5 and installing handlers from native or 68K applications. Updated: 14-May-96 |
