| ---   Q: Can you explain the words `begin` and `again`? When attempting to create a looping structure, the machine hung in my `begin` `again` loop and I could not get out of the loop.  A: `begin` and `again` used together are an infinite loop and are great for debugging hardware on a logic analyzer. Place your test code between these two words. To get out of this loop press Control-Z. |
