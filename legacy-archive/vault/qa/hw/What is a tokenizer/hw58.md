| ---   Q: What is a tokenizer?  A: The tokenizer, defined in Appendix C of IEEE 1275, is a program that converts `FCode` text into `FCode` binary. The tokenizer can be used by developers to convert their source to binary prior to loading that binary in the expansion ROM of their device. |
