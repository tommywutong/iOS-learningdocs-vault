| ---   Q: How do I detect that a menu belongs to a Desk Accessory (DA)?  A: Wow! Your app still supports desk accessories? What a trooper you are. You can detect that a menu belongs to a DA by checking its menu ID; if the ID is less than 0, it belongs to a DA (or other driver).  Further Reference:  [Inside Macintosh: Menu Manager](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/macos8/HumanInterfaceToolbox/MenuManager/menumanager.html)  [Inside Macintosh: Device Manager](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/hardware/DeviceManagers/devicemgr/devicemgr.html) [Dec 22 1998] |
