| Q The documentation says that SndPlayDoubleBuffer doesn't work with 16-bit sounds. Is this true?   A This is no longer the case. Sound Manager 3.0 and later support playing 16-bit sounds with SndPlayDoubleBuffer. SndPlayDoubleBuffer also supports the playing of compressed sounds when you use the SndDoubleBufferHeader2 structure. [Jun 01 1996] |
