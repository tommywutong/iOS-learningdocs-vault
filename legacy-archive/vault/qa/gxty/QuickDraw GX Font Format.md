| ---   Q: Which Apple Developer's CD has the most recent QuickDraw GX font format description?  A: This is not currently available on the Developer CDs. It is available from APDA (part #R0601LL/A). To place an order or get a free APDA Tools catalog call APDA at 800 282-2732.  The QuickDraw GX SDK has a few files that may help. The QuickDraw GX SDK is on the MacOS SDK that was sent out to all Partners in our program. It is also available through APDA. |
