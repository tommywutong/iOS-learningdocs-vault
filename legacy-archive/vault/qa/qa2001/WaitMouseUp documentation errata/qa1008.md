| ---   Q: Why does `WaitMouseUp` return true when the mouse is not up?  A: Because that's the way it was designed and implemented.  The Inside Macintosh documentation found at [`WaitMouseUp`(IM:Tb)](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/mac/Toolbox/Toolbox-75.html) is just plain wrong.  `WaitMouseUp` returns true until the mouse button goes up.   ---  [Feb 27 2001] |
