| ---   Q: Is there an alternative to using JDBC with FrontBase and WebObjects 5.0?  A: Yes. FrontBase has actually provided an adaptor themselves that is native to their database and designed for use with WebObjects 5.0. It can be downloaded from their website at:     [http://www.frontbase.com/cgi-bin/WebObjects/FrontBase](http://www.frontbase.com/cgi-bin/WebObjects/FrontBase).   ---  [Jul 09 2001] |
