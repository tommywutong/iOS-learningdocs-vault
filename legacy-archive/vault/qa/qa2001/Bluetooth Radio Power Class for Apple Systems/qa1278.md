| ---   Q: What are the preferred Bluetooth radio power specifications for Apple Macintosh systems?  A: Apple follows the Bluetooth Transmitter Characteristics laid out in the [Bluetooth Specification Version 1.1](https://www.bluetooth.org/account/login.php?return_to=/docman2/MailDocument.php?group_id%3D53%26document_content_id%3D8150) for power class 2. Bluetooth Class 2 device provides a maximum transmitter output power of 4dBm. This yields about 10 meters of range.  Apple promotes the use of Bluetooth power class 2 radios, rather than a more powerful class 1 radio, to support Bluetooth development as a "cable replacement' solution for peripherals.
