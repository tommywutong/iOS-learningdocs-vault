| ---   Q: When I print with background printing enabled, and print monitor fires up and tries to post an error, my app hangs and no dialog ever appears. What gives?  A: Make sure the bits in your `SIZE` resource are set correctly. This behavior appears when you've got your "Multifinder aware" bit set, but not your "accepts Suspend/Resume events" bit set. |
