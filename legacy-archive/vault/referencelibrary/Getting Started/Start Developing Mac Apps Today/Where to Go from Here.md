# Where to Go from Here

Congratulations on visiting all the stations in _Start Developing Mac Apps Today_ and reading the required companion articles. Now you are ready to begin developing Mac apps. To become an efficient and productive Mac developer, however, you should first [enroll in the Mac Developer Program](https://developer.apple.com/programs/mac/). Next, you might download a sample app project from the OS X Developer Library to see how everything fits together, and then deepen the knowledge and skills you have just acquired.

## Read These Documents to Deepen Your Knowledge of Mac App Development

The following documents are essential reading for any developer of Mac apps:

- _[Mac OS X Technology Overview](../../../documentation/Mac%20OSX/Mac%20Technology%20Overview/About%20Developing%20for%20Mac.md#apple-f4xwc4dqnrsv64tfmyxwi33df52wszbpkridimbqgaytanrx)_ describes the frameworks and other technologies that are available to your app in OS X.
- _OS X Human Interface Guidelines_ teaches you how to make your app consistent with the user interface conventions for OS X.
- _App Distribution Guide_ walks you through the process of submitting apps to the Mac App Store.
- _[Programming with Objective-C](../../../documentation/Cocoa/Programming%20with%20Objective-C/About%20Objective-C.md#apple-f4xwc4dqnrsv64tfmyxwi33df52wszbpkridimbqgeytemjq)_ describes how to define classes, send messages, encapsulate data, and accomplish a variety of other tasks with the Objective-C programming language.
- _[Mac App Programming Guide](../../../documentation/General/Mac%20App%20Programming%20Guide/About%20OS%20X%20App%20Design.md#apple-f4xwc4dqnrsv64tfmyxwi33df52wszbpkridimbqgeydknbt)_ explains the essential things you must know and do when developing a Mac app.
