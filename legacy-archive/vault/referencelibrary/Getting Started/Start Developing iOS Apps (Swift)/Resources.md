## Where to Go from Here

In _Start Developing iOS Apps (Swift)_, you learned the basics of iOS app development. At this point, you’re ready to start developing your first full-featured app. Although taking an app from a simple concept to the App Store isn’t a small task, the process and practices you’ve learned in this document will guide you in the right direction.

Here are a few pointers on where to go from here:

- __Register as a developer.__ _App Distribution Quick Start_ walks you through the process of registering as an Apple developer.
- __Learn to design beautiful app interfaces.__ _iOS Human Interface Guidelines_ teaches you how to make your app consistent with the user interface conventions for iOS. _[Auto Layout Guide](../../../documentation/User%20Experience/Auto%20Layout%20Guide/index.md#apple-f4xwc4dqnrsv64tfmyxwi33df52wszbpkridimbqgeydqnjt)_ teaches you how to create adaptive, versatile user interfaces.
- __Learn the Swift programming language.__ _The Swift Programming Language_ describes everything you need to know about Swift.
- __Learn to develop great apps.__ _[App Programming Guide for iOS](../../../documentation/iPhone/App%20Programming%20Guide%20for%20iOS/About%20iOS%20App%20Architecture.md#apple-f4xwc4dqnrsv64tfmyxwi33df52wszbpkridimbqga3tanzs)_ explains the essential things you must know and do when developing an iOS app.
- __Learn about the technologies available to you.__ _iOS Technology Overview_ describes the frameworks and other technologies that are available to your app in iOS.
- __Debug and test your app.__ _[Debugging with Xcode](../../../documentation/Developer%20Tools/Debugging%20with%20Xcode/About%20Debugging%20with%20Xcode.md#apple-f4xwc4dqnrsv64tfmyxwi33df52wszbpkridimbqge2tamrs)_ teaches you how to thoroughly debug and test your app in Xcode.
- __Ship your app.__ _App Distribution Guide_ walks you through the process of provisioning devices for testing and submitting apps to the App Store.
