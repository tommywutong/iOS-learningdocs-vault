## Getting More Information

To get an overview of the WebObjects platform, you should
read the book _WebObjects Overview_. You can
find general information about WebObjects at
[http://www.apple.com/developer/webobjects](http://www.apple.com/developer/webobjects).

These are other WebObjects documents that provide helpful
information:

- _Installation
  Guide_ includes system requirements, compatibility information,
  and installation instructions. (The _Installation Guide_ is
  included in your WebObjects box.)
- _Post-Installation Instructions_ lists
  tasks that you must perform after installing WebObjects Deployment
  to confirm that the installation was successful and to tailor it for
  your specific needs.
