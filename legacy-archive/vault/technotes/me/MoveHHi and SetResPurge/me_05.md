[Mar 01 1987] |       ---      Introduction When `MoveHHi` is called, even though the handle's block is not actually being purged, the resource data specified by the handle will be written out. An application can prevent this by calling `SetResPurge(FALSE)` before calling `MoveHHi` (and then calling `SetResPurge(TRUE)` after the `MoveHHi` call).
