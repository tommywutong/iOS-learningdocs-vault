The QTKit framework is a set of Cocoa classes and methods designed to support professional-level video and audio capture, and pro-grade recording of media. This project demonstrates how to build a simple, yet powerful, capture application with less than 30 lines of Objective-C code. For a tutorial of the design and building of the app, look in the "QTKit Application Tutorial" document for the section titled "Building a Simple QTKit Recorder Application" or in the "QTKit Application Programming Guide" for the section titled "Creating a Simple Capture and Recording Application".
