# Popover Controllers in iOS

"Popovers" shows how to use UIPopoverController in iOS, including presentation, dismissal, and rotation of popovers. The sample uses a UISplitViewController to show how to present popovers from bar button items. It also demonstrates how you can ensure that multiple UIPopoverControllers are never presented at the same time.
