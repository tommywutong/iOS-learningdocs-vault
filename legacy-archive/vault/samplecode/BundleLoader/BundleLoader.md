"BundleLoader" is a Cocoa application that demonstrates how to load multiple Cocoa bundles or NSBundles (also known as plug-ins) into the main NSApplication. This sample is designed to give you an overall demonstration on how to design, implement, search, load and run multiple plugins into your application.
