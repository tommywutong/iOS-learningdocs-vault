Demonstrates using QTPixelBufferContextCreate with NewMovieFromProperties to output pixel buffers from a movie, draws on the produced buffers then creates CGImages and renders them into an HIImageView.
See Q&A1443 Using QTPixelBufferContextCreate with NewMovieFromProperties for additional information.
