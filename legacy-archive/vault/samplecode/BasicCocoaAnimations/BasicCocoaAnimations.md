"BasicCocoaAnimations" demonstrates the use of the animator proxy to easily animate Cocoa views and windows. The frame of the main window and the opacity of the inspector window animates. Switching between toolbar items changes the view in the window with a basic transition animation. The first two toolbar panels demonstrate animating one or multiple views.
