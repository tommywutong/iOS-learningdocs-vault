## Binding the Dynamic Elements in the Table

- Select __currentGuest.guestName__ in the object browser and drag the cursor to the center of the WOString in the first column to bind its __value__ attribute.
- Similarly, bind __currentGuest.email__ and __currentGuest.comments__ to the second and third WOStrings.
