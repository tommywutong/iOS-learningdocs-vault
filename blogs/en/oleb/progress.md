How long until we see cars where the driver/customer cannot access the engine at all? If Apple made cars, the hood would be secured with [pentalobe screws](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_screw_drives#Pentalobe) (Please visit an authorized garage to have your wiper fluid refilled). I’m not sure if this would be a good thing for cars but in computers, I’ll gladly take better design and usability in a smaller and lighter package over upgradeability and serviceability.

Thanks to my colleague [Jörg Jacobsen](http://www.joergjacobsen.com) for pointing out the striking visual similarity between the innards of the new Macbook Pro and a modern car engine.

**Update June 20, 2012:** Several readers have told me that the short-lived [Audi A2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Audi_a2) came with a hood that was not meant to be opened by the driver although it could be. In its place, the A2 had a small [service hatch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Audi_a2#Service_Hatch_.2F_.22Serviceklappe.22) where oil and wiper fluid could be checked and refilled.
