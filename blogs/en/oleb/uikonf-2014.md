# UIKonf 2014

Tickets for next year’s [UIKonf](http://www.uikonf.com), an English-language iOS developer conference, have gone on sale a few days ago. UIKonf 2014 will take place in Berlin on May 14–16, 2014.

This year’s UIKonf was already a blast, despite the very short lead time my friends Chris, Matt and Peter had given themselves for organizing it. Not only were the talks very good, but the venue, food etc. was all top-notch, as well. And with just over 120 attendees (IIRC), the atmosphere was very relaxed.

I have no doubt that UIKonf 2014 will get even better. And Berlin is always worth a visit, of course. I recommend you get a ticket if it fits your schedule.
