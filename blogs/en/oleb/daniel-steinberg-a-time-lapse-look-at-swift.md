# Daniel Steinberg: A Time Lapse Look at Swift

![Preview of YouTube video: Daniel Steinberg, A Time Laps Look at Swift](https://oleb.net/media/daniel-steinberg-a-timelapse-look-at-swift-video-preview-2297px.jpg)

<sub>[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kyzN5I-2FoY&index=15&list=PLdr22uU_wISqm9QbnczWxXs9qyuWpSU4k) (23 minutes).  
 [The slides](https://speakerdeck.com/dimsumthinking/a-swift-timelapse-uikonf-2016).  
 [Daniel Steinberg on Twitter](https://twitter.com/dimsumthinking).</sub>

In this very entertaining talk, [Daniel Steinberg](https://twitter.com/dimsumthinking) reflects on Swift’s first two years and how the community reacted to it. The main takeaway for me is that we as a community would do well to strive for less drama and take more time to pause, reflect, and empathize. (Duh!)

I love the pacing of this talk. Daniel controls the audience’s reaction through well-timed pauses and slide transitions. Saying nothing can be way more powerful than repeating what’s already on your slides.
