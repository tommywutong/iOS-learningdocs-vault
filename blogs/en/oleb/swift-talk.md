# Swift Talk

Florian and Chris over at [objc.io](https://www.objc.io/) have a new project: [Swift Talk](https://talk.objc.io/) is a weekly video series where the two discuss specific coding problems and develop their solutions live while you watch. Each episode is about 20 minutes long. Half of the episodes will be free, and the other half costs a $9/month subscription.

As with [the](https://oleb.net/blog/2016/03/advanced-swift/) [books](https://oleb.net/blog/2015/12/core-data-book/), I have a small part in Swift Talk as a technical reviewer of the content, so feel free to blame any errors you find in the videos on me.

I really like Chris and Florian’s conversational approach. The videos are not meant as tutorials for you to follow, but rather discussions of the pros and cons of their solutions, and the live-coding makes it easy to follow their thought process. I believe this can be much more productive for the viewer than being presented with a finished solution.
