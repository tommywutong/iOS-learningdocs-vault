# iOS Development Highlights: October 2010

Here are some of the things from across the iOS blogosphere I found interesting this past month.

- event, which gave us the first glimpse of OS X Lion (including some iOS design patterns) and the Mac App Store. Some opinions from the community:

    - encourages the Mac community to build an open app store on the Mac

      as a counterweight to Apple’s.
    - applauds the Mac App Store but is concerned about Apple’s guidelines for acceptable App Store apps

      (no trials, no refunds).
    - asks Apple to review apps based on their benefit to the users

      and not on bureaucratic rules like no use of private APIs and no need for root privileges.
    - The Mac App Store isn’t for today’s Mac developers

      , but rather for the (probably much larger) group of future OS X developers who are drawn to Mac development by the App Store’s appeal. Most apps will probably be inexpensive and “low-risk”, much like today’s iOS apps.
    - disagrees with Marco

      because in his opinion, users expect more (features, functionality) from desktop apps. Such apps would have to be more expensive and the current app store model (no trials, no upgrade pricing) does not cater for pricier software.
    - hopes that Apple uses the App Store to highlight high-quality software

      instead of going for sheer volume.
