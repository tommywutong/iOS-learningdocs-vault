# Strings in Swift Updated

I have completely rewritten my article on [Strings in Swift](https://oleb.net/blog/2014/07/swift-strings/) from earlier this month. Xcode 6 beta 4 fundamentally changed how the `String` and `Character` type handle Unicode characters that are composed of multiple code points. I consider Swift’s implementation of Unicode a huge step up from what we had with `NSString`. I encourage you to check it out.
