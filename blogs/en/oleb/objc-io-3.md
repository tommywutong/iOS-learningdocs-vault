# objc.io #3

My friends and fellow Berlin-based developers [Chris Eidhof](https://twitter.com/chriseidhof), [Florian Kugler](https://twitter.com/floriankugler) and [Daniel Eggert](https://twitter.com/danielboedewadt) have just published [the third issue of objc.io](http://www.objc.io/issue-3/). objc.io is still a very young platform, but I feel it has already established itself among the best iOS development-related websites.

Each month, the guys pick one topic and then publish several articles that discuss different aspects of the overarching topic. I have already learned a lot of stuff from reading the first two issues.

This month’s issue is all about iOS’s view layer and deals with both very technical and more practical stuff. I am glad to report that I am one of the guest authors in this issue, with an article on [UICollectionView layouts](http://www.objc.io/issue-3/collection-view-layouts.html). The other guest is [Joe Conway](https://twitter.com/joeconwaystk), who wrote about [Understanding Scroll Views](http://www.objc.io/issue-3/scroll-view.html). Check it out!
