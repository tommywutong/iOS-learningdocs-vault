# iOS Development Highlights: July 2010

Unlike myself, the iOS developer community has been quite active posting great content this month. Here are some of the things I found most interesting from across the blogosphere:

- Back in June, John Siracusa talked about the [possiblity of Apple replacing Objective-C with a new platform](http://arstechnica.com/apple/news/2010/06/copland-2010-revisited.ars/) based on an abstracted memory model. In a series of posts ([Surpass](http://waffle.wootest.net/2010/06/19/surpass/), [Not Replaced](http://waffle.wootest.net/2010/07/06/xlang-clarifications/), [Objective Dash](http://waffle.wootest.net/2010/07/16/objective-dash/)) Jesper explored a similar path, and finally Matt Gallagher chimed in: [Is a virtual machine for Cocoa programming inevitable?](http://cocoawithlove.com/2010/07/is-virtual-machine-for-cocoa.html?utm_source=feedburner&utm_medium=feed&utm_campaign=Feed:+CocoaWithLove+(Cocoa+with+Love)) All of these posts are very interesting reads. I certainly hope that Apple is working on leaving Objective-C’s C legacy behind.
- Martin Pilkington about [new features in the Objective-C runtime](http://www.mcubedsw.com/blog/index.php/site/comments/new_objective-c_features/) (called Objective-C 2.2 by Martin). Say goodbye to `@synthesize`!
- [Terminal Tips and Tricks For Mac OS X](http://superuser.com/questions/52483/terminal-tips-and-tricks-for-mac-os-x) is a super-useful collection of tricks that recently made its way around Twitter. My favorites are [opensnoop](http://superuser.com/questions/52483/terminal-tips-and-tricks-for-mac-os-x/52571#52571) and [mdfind -live](http://superuser.com/questions/52483/terminal-tips-and-tricks-for-mac-os-x/60487#60487) (via [Christian Beer](http://chbeer.de/)).
- [ShareKit](http://www.getsharekit.com/) is an open-source library that enables your iOS app to share content on Twitter, Facebook, etc. with just a few lines of code.
- [Seriously](https://github.com/probablycorey/seriously) is a new HTTP library with an easy-to-use block-based interface (via [Joost Schuur](http://iosdevgoodies.joostschuur.com/seriously-http-objective-c-library)).
- It never hurts to have another pull-to-refresh implementation (made famous by Tweetie 2) so [Leah Culver has written one](http://blog.leahculver.com/2010/07/iphone-pull-to-refresh.html).
- The ever-resourceful Matt Gemmell developed the `UISplitViewController` Apple should have made: [MGSplitViewController](http://mattgemmell.com/2010/07/31/mgsplitviewcontroller-for-ipad) can display the split view in landscape and portrait, has a vertical and a horizontal layout, and interactive sizing of the master and detail panes.
