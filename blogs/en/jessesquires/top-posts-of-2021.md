To finish off this year, here are my most popular posts of 2021. You can find previous years [here under the #top-posts tag](https://www.jessesquires.com/blog/tags/top-posts/). As you likely know, I use [GoatCounter for analytics](https://www.jessesquires.com/blog/2020/05/08/simple-private-opensource-analytics-with-goatcounter/) for my site. (You should [check it out](https://www.goatcounter.com)!) All of my analytics data is [publicly available](https://stats.jessesquires.com) so you can view it too.
