To continue my (almost) tradition of sharing my top posts, here are my most popular posts of 2023. You can find previous years [here under the #top-posts tag](https://www.jessesquires.com/blog/tags/top-posts/). Last year, 2022, is notably absent from this series — I was too burnt out last year (for a number of reasons) to write one of these posts. So as a bonus, I’ll include my top posts of 2022 here as well! As usual, all of my analytics data is [publicly available](https://stats.jessesquires.com), made possible by the excellent [GoatCounter Analytics](https://www.goatcounter.com), so you can view it too.

I would like thank everyone who continues to read my blog. I truly appreciate being able to share my writing with all of you.
