## [Queue, Queeu, Quuee](https://belkadan.com/blog/2019/09/Queue-Queeu-Quuee/?tag=humor)

25 September 2019

- queue /ˈkju/: first-in first-out
- queeu /ˈki.ju/: last-in first-out
- quuee /kuˈwi/: general interface, order unspecified
