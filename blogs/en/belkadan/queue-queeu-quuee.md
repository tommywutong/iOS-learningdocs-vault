## [Queue, Queeu, Quuee](#)

PRO TIP: the letters in “queue” describe the order elements are accessed.

- queue /ˈkju/: first-in first-out
- queeu /ˈki.ju/: last-in first-out
- quuee /kuˈwi/: general interface, order unspecified

See also “quay” /ki/, my least favorite spelling in the English language.

---

Further data structures per [Nikolai Vazquez](https://nikolaivazquez.com):

- que /kɛ/: a write-only data structure
- q /kʊ/: a permanently-empty collection
