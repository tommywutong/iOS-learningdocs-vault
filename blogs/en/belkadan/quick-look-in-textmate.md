## [Quick Look in TextMate](#)

Yes, I’m one of those coders who still uses [TextMate](http://macromates.com), the four-year-old plaintext editor for Mac OS X. And when I say “four-year-old”, I mean it’s been four years since a major update. (Every now and then point updates are still pushed out, but TextMate 2 is the “Duke Nukem Forever” of the Mac text editor land. [Oh wait](http://dukenukemforever.com).)

Anyway, I was browsing around for [interesting](https://github.com/jezdez/textmate-missingdrawer) [plug-ins](https://github.com/briancollins/nice_find) and came across one that purported to provide Quick Look functionality to the files in the project outline. Unfortunately, it wasn’t compatible with Snow Leopard. So, what’s a plug-in hacker to do? [Make my own](https://github.com/belkadan/textmate-quicklook)!

If you want to add Quick Look to your TextMate projects, [download the plugin](https://github.com/downloads/belkadan/textmate-quicklook/TMQuickLook.tmplugin.zip) (zipped) to `~/Library/Application Support/TextMate/PlugIns` and relaunch TextMate. (You should create that folder if it doesn’t already exist.)

Bug reports welcome!
