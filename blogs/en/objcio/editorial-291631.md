> 原文：[Editorial](https://www.objc.io/issues/20-interviews/editorial)　·　objc.io

Happy New Year and welcome to objc.io issue 20!

To mark the first issue of the year, we did things a bit differently, just like last year. This time, we interviewed a few developers who had interesting stories to tell. We hope you will enjoy reading what they have to say.

We want to thank [Andy Matuschak](https://www.objc.io/issues/20-interviews/andy-matuschak/), [Loren Brichter](https://www.objc.io/issues/20-interviews/loren-brichter/), and [Natasha Murashev](https://www.objc.io/issues/20-interviews/natasha-murashev/) for being a part of this special issue.

Best from Berlin,

Chris, Daniel, and Florian.
