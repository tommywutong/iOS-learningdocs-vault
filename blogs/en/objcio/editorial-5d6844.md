> 原文：[Editorial](https://www.objc.io/issues/15-testing/editorial)　·　objc.io

Hi all,

This month's issue is about a topic we've wanted to cover for a long time: Testing.

Automated testing is a disputed topic; some people love it, while other people hate it. But testing has many different aspects, and we hope that this issue — no matter what camp you're in — will offer something that helps you improve the quality of your projects.

We will take look at the umbrella term of 'testing' from many different perspectives to give you a broad overview of all the different techniques involved. We start out with a look at what [behavior-driven development](https://www.objc.io/issues/15-testing/behavior-driven-development/) is about and how to use it. Then we'll explore learnings from using Apple's [XCTest toolchain](https://www.objc.io/issues/15-testing/xctest/). Next, we will dive into [dependency injection](https://www.objc.io/issues/15-testing/dependency-injection/) and how it relates to testing. If you've ever wondered about [bad testing practices](https://www.objc.io/issues/15-testing/bad-testing-practices/), we have you covered, too. [Mocking](https://www.objc.io/issues/15-testing/mocking-stubbing/) is another common tool in the toolbox when writing tests, so we'll take a look at that. Finally, we finish off with two articles about testing related to user interface code: [user interface testing](https://www.objc.io/issues/15-testing/user-interface-testing/) and [snapshot testing](https://www.objc.io/issues/15-testing/snapshot-testing/). Thanks to all the authors for contributing their experience in this field!

Happy testing,

Chris, Daniel, and Florian.
