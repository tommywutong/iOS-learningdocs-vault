# Joining the Open Source Pledge

September 6, 2024 by

[Josh Cohenzadeh](https://twitter.com/jshchnz)

Open Source

![Emerge Tools Open Source Pledge](https://www.emergetools.com/_next/image?url=%2F_next%2Fstatic%2Fmedia%2Femerge-open-source-pledge.d6a52ff7.png&w=3840&q=100&dpl=dpl_9GUvYRYkSXVcp9398feaiSHuz7SE)

At Emerge Tools, we recognize that our success is built on the shoulders of countless open-source contributors. Our products leverage a diverse ecosystem of open-source frameworks, libraries, and tools that form the backbone of modern software development.

While we've always been committed to giving back by open-sourcing some of [our own tools](https://github.com/EmergeTools), we're taking a step further in our commitment to the open-source community. We're excited to announce that we've joined the [Open Source Pledge](https://osspledge.com/), an initiative that formalizes our financial support for the projects that superpowers our work.

As part of this pledge, we've already contributed $18,063 towards open-source in 2024, surpassing our initial goal of $2,000 per full-time developer & plan to maintain that standard into the future.

## [Open-Source We're Supporting](https://www.emergetools.com/blog/posts/emerge-joining-open-source-pledge#open-source-we-re-supporting)

## [Our Commitment to Open-Source](https://www.emergetools.com/blog/posts/emerge-joining-open-source-pledge#our-commitment-to-open-source)

We're also committed to contributing to the open-source community through our own projects:

- [Open-Source Hacker News](https://www.emergetools.com/blog/posts/open-sourcing-emerge-tools-hackernews-app): An entirely free, open-source Hacker News app built using the latest and greatest in Android dev.
- [ETTrace](https://github.com/EmergeTools/ETTrace): Easily and accurately profile iOS apps. Locally measure performance of your app, without Xcode or Instruments.
- [Pow](https://github.com/EmergeTools/Pow): Delightful SwiftUI effects for your app.
- [ETSymbolication](https://github.com/EmergeTools/ETSymbolication): Generate symbols for frameworks like SwiftUI to symbolicate crash reports.
- [SmallStrings](https://github.com/EmergeTools/SmallStrings): Reduce localized .strings file sizes by 80%.
- [SnapshotPreviews](https://github.com/EmergeTools/SnapshotPreviews-iOS): An all-in-one iOS snapshot testing solution.
