# AVCaptureCameraLensSmudgeDetectionStatus.smudgeNotDetected

<sub>Case</sub>

Indicates that the most recent detection found no smudge on the camera lens.

<sub>iOS, iPadOS, Mac Catalyst, macOS, tvOS</sub>

```swift
case smudgeNotDetected
```

## See Also

### Status values

- [AVCaptureCameraLensSmudgeDetectionStatusDisabled](disabled.md) — Indicates that the detection is not enabled.
- [AVCaptureCameraLensSmudgeDetectionStatusSmudged](smudged.md) — Indicates that the most recent detection found the camera lens to be smudged.
- [AVCaptureCameraLensSmudgeDetectionStatusUnknown](unknown.md) — Indicates that the detection result has not settled, commonly caused by excessive camera movement or the content of the scene.
