# angle

<sub>Instance Property</sub>

The angle of the accessory’s position, measured in radians clockwise from the top of the primary pointer.

<sub>iOS, iPadOS, Mac Catalyst, tvOS, visionOS, watchOS</sub>

```objc
CGFloat angle;
```
