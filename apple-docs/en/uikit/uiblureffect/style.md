### Additional styles

- [UIBlurEffectStyleExtraLight](style/extralight.md) — The area of the view is lighter than the underlying view.
- [UIBlurEffectStyleLight](style/light.md) — The area of the view is the same approximate lightness of the underlying view.
- [UIBlurEffectStyleDark](style/dark.md) — The area of the view is darker than the underlying view.
- [UIBlurEffectStyleExtraDark](style/extradark.md) — The area of the view is even more dark than the underlying view.
- [UIBlurEffectStyleRegular](style/regular.md) — A regular blur style that adapts to the user interface style.
