# Horizontal alignment modes

<sub>API Collection</sub>

These constants are used by the [alignmentMode](catextlayer/alignmentmode.md) property.

## Topics

### Constants

- [kCAAlignmentNatural](catextlayeralignmentmode/natural.md) — Use the natural alignment of the text’s script.
- [kCAAlignmentLeft](catextlayeralignmentmode/left.md) — Text is visually left aligned.
- [kCAAlignmentRight](catextlayeralignmentmode/right.md) — Text is visually right aligned.
- [kCAAlignmentCenter](catextlayeralignmentmode/center.md) — Text is visually center aligned.
- [kCAAlignmentJustified](catextlayeralignmentmode/justified.md) — Text is justified.
