# Emitter Modes

<sub>API Collection</sub>

These constants specify the possible emitter modes. They are used by the [emitterMode](caemitterlayer/emittermode.md) property.

## Topics

### Constants

- [kCAEmitterLayerPoints](caemitterlayeremittermode/points.md) — Particles are emitted from points on the particle emitter.
- [kCAEmitterLayerOutline](caemitterlayeremittermode/outline.md) — Particles are emitted from the outline of the particle emitter.
- [kCAEmitterLayerSurface](caemitterlayeremittermode/surface.md) — Particles are emitted from the surface of the particle emitter.
- [kCAEmitterLayerVolume](caemitterlayeremittermode/volume.md) — Particles are emitted from the a position within the particle emitter.

## See Also

### Constants

- [Emitter Shape](emitter-shape.md) — The emission shape is a one, two or three dimensional shape that defines where the emitted particles originate. The shapes are defined by a subset of [emitterPosition](caemitterlayer/emitterposition.md), [emitterZPosition](caemitterlayer/emitterzposition.md), [emitterSize](caemitterlayer/emittersize.md) and [emitterDepth](caemitterlayer/emitterdepth.md) properties.
- [Emitter Render Order](emitter-render-order.md) — These constants specify the order that emitter cells are composited. They are used by the [renderMode](caemitterlayer/rendermode.md) property.
