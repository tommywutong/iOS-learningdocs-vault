# Common Transition Subtypes

<sub>API Collection</sub>

These constants specify the direction of motion-based transitions. They are used with the [subtype](catransition/subtype.md) property.

## Topics

### Constants

- [kCATransitionFromRight](catransitionsubtype/fromright.md) — The transition begins at the right side of the layer.
- [kCATransitionFromLeft](catransitionsubtype/fromleft.md) — The transition begins at the left side of the layer.
- [kCATransitionFromTop](catransitionsubtype/fromtop.md) — The transition begins at the top of the layer.
- [kCATransitionFromBottom](catransitionsubtype/frombottom.md) — The transition begins at the bottom of the layer.
