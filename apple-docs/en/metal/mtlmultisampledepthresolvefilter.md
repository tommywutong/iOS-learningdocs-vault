## Topics

### Depth resolve filters

- [MTLMultisampleDepthResolveFilterSample0](mtlmultisampledepthresolvefilter/sample0.md) — No filter is applied.
- [MTLMultisampleDepthResolveFilterMin](mtlmultisampledepthresolvefilter/min.md) — The GPU compares all depth samples in the pixel and selects the sample with the smallest value.
- [MTLMultisampleDepthResolveFilterMax](mtlmultisampledepthresolvefilter/max.md) — The GPU compares all depth samples in the pixel and selects the sample with the largest value.
