# scrolls

<sub>Type Property</sub>

A behavior that prioritizes scrolling the content of a presentation when swiping, rather than resizing the presentation.

<sub>iOS, iPadOS, Mac Catalyst, macOS, tvOS, visionOS, watchOS</sub>

