### Gesture input options

- [directTouch](gestureinputkinds/directtouch.md) — A person is touching content directly. _(beta)_

- [pencil](gestureinputkinds/pencil.md) — A person is touching content directly with an Apple Pencil, or an other supported pencil device. _(beta)_
