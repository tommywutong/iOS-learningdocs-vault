# Technology Overviews

Learn about the wide range of technologies you use to develop software for Apple platforms.

## Overview

Whether you’re new to Apple platforms or you’ve been working with them for years, finding the technology you need is an important first step. With so many technologies available to you, it’s sometimes difficult to know where to start. The following topics offer a high-level view of the technologies available to you, and guidance about which technologies you might choose to solve particular problems.

## Get started

Start your exploration with the foundational technologies you use to build your app or game, and make your interface shine by adopting Liquid Glass.

- [App design and UI](technologyoverviews/app-design-and-ui.md)
- [Games](technologyoverviews/games.md)

## Discover Apple technologies

Explore the technologies you use to build your app’s unique experience. Whether you’re buiding core features like your app’s data model, accessing device-specific hardware, making your app more intelligent, or adopting features unique to Apple devices, Apple frameworks help you do so easily and efficiently.

- [Data management](technologyoverviews/data-management.md)
- [Core experiences](technologyoverviews/core-experiences.md)
- [Apple Intelligence and machine learning](technologyoverviews/ai-machine-learning.md)
- [Audio and video](technologyoverviews/audio-and-video.md)
- [Graphics, drawing, and animation](technologyoverviews/graphics-drawing-and-animation.md)
- [Tools and distribution](technologyoverviews/tools-and-distribution.md)
- [Hardware, networking, and sensors](technologyoverviews/hardware-networking-sensors.md)

## Explore the Apple Developer Documentation

Consult the [Apple Developer Documentation](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/) for in-depth information about individual technologies. The developer documentation includes API reference, articles, sample code, and tutorials to help you learn how to use a given technology. It also offers guidance about the best way to solve specific challenges.

## Topics

### Get started

- [App design and UI](technologyoverviews/app-design-and-ui.md) — Choose a programming approach to build your app, create your app’s interface, and implement the fundamental behaviors that your app requires.
- [Games](technologyoverviews/games.md) — Deliver fantastic game experiences to millions of players worldwide using Apple hardware, graphics, audio, social gaming, and distribution tools.

### Discover Apple technologies

- [Data management](technologyoverviews/data-management.md) — Build your app’s data model, persist that data to disk or iCloud, and access people’s personal data.
- [Core experiences](technologyoverviews/core-experiences.md) — Embrace features that improve people’s experience with your app on Apple devices.
- [Apple Intelligence and machine learning](technologyoverviews/ai-machine-learning.md) — Add intelligent features with Apple Intelligence, machine learning, and related technologies.
- [Audio and video](technologyoverviews/audio-and-video.md) — Support audio and video content in your app.
- [Graphics, drawing, and animation](technologyoverviews/graphics-drawing-and-animation.md) — Create 2D and 3D content for your app, and learn how to animate and print that content.
- [Tools and distribution](technologyoverviews/tools-and-distribution.md) — Create software on Apple platforms using Xcode and the Swift programming language, and distribute your apps on the App Store.
- [Hardware, networking, and sensors](technologyoverviews/hardware-networking-sensors.md) — Access device-related sensors, connect to a network, and build low-level drivers and software to support custom hardware.
